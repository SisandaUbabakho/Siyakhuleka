Quick guidance

Security incident

Act quickly

Report suspected malware, phishing, lost devices or data exposure to the service desk immediately. Do not delete evidence or reconnect an isolated device.

Network outage

Describe the impact

When raising a ticket, state your site, the service affected, when it started and whether other colleagues are affected.

Policy status

Check the version

Before following a procedure, check its owner, approval status, version and review date on the Document Control page.
