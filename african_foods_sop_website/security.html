IT Security Policy Guidelines

Recommended minimum security rules for employees, contractors and authorised IT staff across all AFC locations.

Security guidelines

  • Use an individual account. Never share passwords. Use long, unique passwords and multi-factor authentication (MFA) wherever available.
  • Only access files, applications and systems needed for your job. Restricted systems, including ERP and network administration, require authorised access.
  • Managers must approve new accounts and access changes. Notify IT promptly when employees join, change roles or leave.
  • Keep company computers updated and protected by approved endpoint security and firewall controls. Do not disable security settings.
  • Use approved software only. Do not install unlicensed applications, unknown tools or unauthorised remote-access software.
  • Keep business, customer, employee and financial information in approved storage locations. Share it only with authorised recipients.
  • Use company-approved Wi-Fi and network connections. Do not connect unauthorised routers, switches or access points.
  • Site-to-site VPNs and network equipment must be configured only by authorised IT staff using approved procedures.
  • Do not open suspicious email attachments, follow unexpected login links or approve unrecognised MFA prompts. Report suspected phishing.
  • Report lost devices, suspected malware, unusual account activity, accidental data disclosure or network incidents immediately.
  • Keep server and network equipment in restricted areas. Visitors must be supervised in secure IT spaces.
  • Backups and configuration files must be protected from unauthorised access and tested through planned restoration exercises.
  • Security exceptions require a documented business reason, risk assessment, authorised approval and review date.

What to do if you suspect an incident

  1. Stop the activity that may be unsafe.
  2. Contact the service desk immediately and provide the device, site, time and symptoms.
  3. Do not delete files, wipe the device or investigate beyond your authorisation.
  4. Follow IT instructions and keep relevant messages or error details available.
Do not put passwords or VPN keys in help desk tickets. IT should use approved secure channels for secrets.

Owner: IT Manager · Status: Proposed — requires management approval · Suggested review: annually and after a significant incident.
