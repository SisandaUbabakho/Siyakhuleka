IT Acquisition Procedures & Approvals

Use this procedure for hardware, software, licences, connectivity and IT services.

Purchase workflow

  1. Submit a request. State the business need, quantity, location, required date and cost centre.
  2. Obtain manager review. The line manager confirms the need, priority and available budget.
  3. Request technical assessment. IT checks compatibility, security, supportability, licensing, warranty and integration with AFC’s existing network.
  4. Compare suppliers. Procurement obtains quotations according to company rules and records supplier comparisons.
  5. Check lifecycle and risk. Consider total cost, renewals, maintenance, supplier reliability and end-of-support dates.
  6. Get formal approval. The authorised budget holder approves before an order is placed. Higher-value purchases follow management’s delegated authority rules.
  7. Issue a purchase order. Procurement creates the approved order. Staff must not commit AFC to a supplier before approval.
  8. Receive and test. IT checks delivery against the order and tests the equipment or service.
  9. Secure and register. Apply updates and security settings, assign an asset tag, and record serial numbers, licences, warranty, location and assigned user.
  10. Match the invoice. Finance/procurement checks the invoice against the purchase order and accepted delivery before payment.
  11. Review. Record supplier, warranty, compatibility or performance issues for future decisions.

Emergency purchases

If delay creates a serious operational or security risk, the IT Manager may request expedited approval from the authorised manager. Record the reason, approver, cost and supporting documents. Use AFC’s official monetary thresholds; this demonstration does not invent them.

Required request information

  • Requestor and department/site
  • Business purpose and impact if not approved
  • Item/service and quantity
  • Required date and estimated cost
  • Manager approval and cost centre
  • IT compatibility/security assessment

Process owner: IT Manager with Procurement and Finance · Status: Proposed — confirm approval limits before use.
