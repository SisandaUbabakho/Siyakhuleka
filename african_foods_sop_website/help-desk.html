Service Desk / Help Desk Procedures

The service desk is the central point for IT support across Cape Town, Durban, Gqeberha and Polokwane.

How to log a ticket

  1. Use the organisation’s approved ticketing system or designated support contact. If unavailable, use the approved backup contact method.
  2. Include your name, site, contact details, device/service, time the issue began, symptoms, error message and business impact.
  3. Do not include passwords, VPN keys or other secrets in a ticket.
  4. The service desk assigns a ticket number, priority, owner and status and confirms receipt.

Proposed priority and initial response targets

These are recommendations for the WIL project, not confirmed existing service commitments. Management should approve them before use.

Initial troubleshooting

  • Check whether one user, one site or multiple sites are affected.
  • For basic connectivity, check power, cable/link lights, Wi-Fi connection and whether the device has a network address.
  • For a branch-wide issue, escalate to the network administrator to check the router/firewall, VPN and ISP status.
  • For ERP problems, report whether the service is unreachable or the application itself is showing an error. The network design identifies the ERP/database server as 10.10.90.10.
  • Do not make unauthorised changes to firewalls, VPNs, VLANs, DHCP or production servers.

Escalation and closure

  1. Escalate security, server, VPN and multi-site issues promptly.
  2. Record tests, actions, approvals and the outcome in the ticket.
  3. Confirm service restoration with the user before closing.
  4. For repeated problems, record the likely root cause and recommend a permanent fix.
Security incident? Report it immediately. Do not delete evidence, wipe a device or reconnect an isolated device without IT instruction.

Owner: Service Desk Lead / IT Manager · Status: Proposed — confirm targets and official contact details before launch.
