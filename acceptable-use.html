Acceptable Use Policy

This policy describes how employees and authorised users should use AFC’s IT equipment, network, email, applications and information.

Acceptable use

  • Use company systems primarily for authorised business activities.
  • Lock your screen when leaving a workstation unattended.
  • Store company information only in approved locations and follow confidentiality requirements.
  • Use software and cloud services approved by IT and properly licensed.
  • Request access, software, equipment and technical changes through the approved process.
  • Only authorised staff may publish or change public website content.
  • Report lost equipment, suspicious activity, service interruptions and accidental data disclosure promptly.

Prohibited use

  • Sharing passwords, using another person’s account or attempting to access information without permission.
  • Installing pirated, unlicensed or unapproved software.
  • Disabling security tools, updates, firewalls or monitoring controls.
  • Using company systems for fraud, harassment, unlawful activity or activities that create avoidable legal or reputational risk.
  • Sending confidential information to personal email or unauthorised file-sharing services.
  • Connecting personal routers, switches or other equipment to the company network without approval.
  • Scanning, intercepting or changing network traffic without written authorisation.
  • Excessive personal streaming or downloads that interfere with work or network performance.

Monitoring and consequences

Company systems may be monitored for legitimate security, service reliability and compliance purposes, subject to applicable law and company policy. Violations may lead to access restrictions, investigation or disciplinary action under the applicable company process.

Owner: IT Manager · Status: Proposed — requires management approval · Suggested review: annually.
